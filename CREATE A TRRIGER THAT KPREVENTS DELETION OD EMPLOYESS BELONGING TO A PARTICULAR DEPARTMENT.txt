12. CREATE A TRRIGER THAT KPREVENTS DELETION OD EMPLOYESS BELONGING TO A PARTICULAR DEPARTMENT.


Step 1: Create Trigger

Example: Prevent deletion of employees belonging to Department 10.
CREATE OR REPLACE TRIGGER PREVENT_DELETE
BEFORE DELETE ON EMP
FOR EACH ROW
BEGIN
    IF :OLD.DEPTNO = 10 THEN
        RAISE_APPLICATION_ERROR
        (-20001, 'Deletion Not Allowed');
    END IF;
END;
/


Step 2: Test Trigger


DELETE FROM EMP
WHERE EMPNO = 101;
