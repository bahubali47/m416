11. CRETE A TRIGGER THAR AUTOMATICALLY RECORD THE INSERTION DATE AND USE INFORMATION WHENEVER A NEW EMPLOYEE RECORD IS INSERTE.


Step 1: Add INSERT_DATE and INSERT_USER Columns in EMP Table



ALTER TABLE EMP ADD
(
    INSERT_DATE DATE,
    INSERT_USER VARCHAR2(50)
);


Step 2: Create Trigger


CREATE OR REPLACE TRIGGER EMP_INFO_TRG
BEFORE INSERT ON EMP
FOR EACH ROW
BEGIN
    :NEW.INSERT_DATE := SYSDATE;
    :NEW.INSERT_USER := USER;
END;
/
